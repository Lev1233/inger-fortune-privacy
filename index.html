🔮 Finger Fortune - Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

Introduction

Finger Fortune ("we", "our", or "the app") is an entertainment application that provides fortune readings based on fingerprint scanning, zodiac signs, and palm reading simulations. This Privacy Policy explains what information is handled when you use the app.

Data Stored Only on Your Device

  • Birth date (for zodiac sign calculation)
  • Fortune reading history
  • App preferences and settings

This data is stored locally using encrypted storage. We do not have access to it and it is never transmitted to any server.

Data We Do Not Collect

  • Fingerprint data (biometric checks are handled by your device's system only; the app never receives fingerprint data)
  • Palm photographs (used temporarily on the device and never saved or transmitted)
  • Your name, email address, phone number, or contacts
  • Precise (GPS) location

Third-Party Services

The app uses the following Google services, which collect data automatically as described in their own policies:

Google AdMob (advertising) may collect:

  • Device and advertising identifiers (Android advertising ID, app set ID)
  • IP address, which may be used to estimate your approximate location
  • App interaction data (for example, app launches, taps, ad views)
  • Diagnostic and performance information

This data is used for showing ads, analytics, and fraud prevention. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings.

Firebase Analytics (usage analytics) may collect app interaction data, device identifiers, and approximate location derived from your IP address, to help us understand how the app is used.

Firebase Crashlytics (crash reporting) collects crash logs and diagnostic information (device model, OS version, app state at the time of a crash) to help us fix bugs.

All data sent by these services is encrypted in transit. Learn more:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses data from apps that use its services
  • Firebase Privacy and Security

Children's Privacy

This app is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data Deletion

You can delete all locally stored data at any time using the "Clear Data" option in the app's Settings screen, or by uninstalling the app. To request deletion of data collected by Google services, contact us at the email below or use Google's privacy tools.

Entertainment Disclaimer

Finger Fortune is an entertainment app only. Fortune readings, zodiac analysis, and palm readings are generated randomly and should not be taken as real predictions or advice.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected in the "Last updated" date above.
